\begin{abstract}
What does acceptance mean when a model is written with names but conformance is
decided over internal identities? We give a reusable finite semantics for a
static structural profile of EMOF 2.5.1 and connect three views of a model:
symbolic source, an identity-based core, and an executable decision. Source
satisfaction is defined independently of elaboration. Under an explicit lexical
condition, it is equivalent to successful elaboration followed by checker
acceptance; checker acceptance is in turn equivalent to declarative conformance
for every represented finite input. The account retains inherited declaration
identity, multiplicity, order, duplicate occurrences, opposite ends, and static
containment. A small catalog model illustrates the connection throughout. A
restricted EMF experiment then tests a textual/native realization with explicit
identity evidence on six adapted Train snapshots. The formal result covers the
stated static profile and begins at the source abstract syntax; parsing, EMF
adaptation, reflective behavior, and full EMOF self-description remain outside it.
\keywords{EMOF \and Metamodeling \and Lean \and Model conformance}
\end{abstract}
