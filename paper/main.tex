\begin{abstract}
A reusable foundation for model-driven software engineering requires an explicit
meaning for metamodel declarations and their instances. We formalize a finite
structural fragment of EMOF 2.5.1 in Lean, with source interpretations that remain
separate from implementation proofs. The account distinguishes structural
multiplicity from class-creation prerequisites, occurrence counts from container
objects, and parent uniqueness from active container-role uniqueness. It retains
inherited declaration identity, opposite incidence, ordering and duplicate values.
Mechanized consequences and a complete checker expose this semantics for arbitrary
supported metamodels and finite snapshots. A conditional adequacy theorem connects
independently defined symbolic satisfaction to actual elaboration. An attributed
Train metamodel projection demonstrates structural consequences without claiming
to verify its domain queries. Actual EMF validation cases distinguish agreement
on common obligations from schema restrictions and validator differences. The
foundation supports later work on reflective preservation and richer metadata;
operations, defaults/unset history and full EMOF self-description remain outside
the established fragment.
\keywords{EMOF \and Metamodeling \and Lean \and Model conformance}
\end{abstract}
