\begin{abstract}
Metamodel declarations need precise conformance rules that validation and model
reasoning can share. Deriving these rules from EMOF requires reconciling its
restrictions, adopted UML definitions and reflective-operation contexts. We
formalize a structural subset of EMOF 2.5.1 in Lean, covering inheritance,
properties, occurrence multiplicity, opposites and containment. A source audit
distinguishes creation prerequisites from structural bounds and parent identity
from active container-property identity. We prove that an executable checker
decides the defined conformance relation and that symbolic declarations preserve
and reflect conformance through elaboration under explicit lexical conditions.
A reusable opposite-count consequence rules out repeated-link edits across
candidate snapshots, even for a nonunique forward property. A railway projection,
structural witnesses and native EMF comparisons expose useful behavior, schema
and validator differences, and default-presence limits. The resulting library
supports reasoning over represented metamodels and finite snapshots; reflective
execution, general default/unset state and full EMOF self-description remain
unsupported. Parser and native-adapter correctness are outside the proved boundary.
\keywords{EMOF \and Metamodeling \and Lean \and Model conformance}
\end{abstract}
