\begin{abstract}
EMOF provides structures for defining modeling languages, but its structural
restrictions and reflective-service conditions require different interpretations
when defining conformance. We formalize a finite structural fragment of
EMOF~2.5.1 in Lean. Symbolic declarations receive an independent semantics and
are elaborated into the representation used by an executable checker.
We prove checker soundness and completeness, conditional source adequacy, and
an opposite-count property that excludes repeated links without forward
uniqueness. Comparisons on a Train snapshot with 754 objects and 3,378
observations distinguish aligned loading from an adaptation with twelve
presence differences. Across 45 paired case trials, 40 complete and five
VL-MOF processes time out on the deepest containment chain.
The formalization provides an executable basis for structural conformance and
reusable model reasoning, with explicit distinctions between standards
interpretation, mechanized guarantees and native behavior. It covers static
snapshots; reflective execution, general default/unset semantics, and correctness
of parsing and native interchange remain outside the proved scope.
\keywords{EMOF \and Metamodeling \and Formal semantics \and Model conformance \and Mechanized verification}
\end{abstract}
