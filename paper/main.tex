\begin{abstract}
EMOF supplies structural modeling constructs, but conformance depends on
interpreting its restrictions separately from reflective-service conditions.
We formalize a finite structural fragment of EMOF~2.5.1 in Lean, with independent
symbolic semantics and an executable checker. We prove checker soundness and
completeness on raw inputs, conditional AST-to-Core adequacy, an opposite-count
constraint, and preservation of structural conformance under a Boolean-slot edit.
A full Train walkthrough reproduces a retained schema and 754-object,
3,378-observation snapshot through tested DSL elaboration and exercises accepted
and rejected edits. Native comparisons distinguish aligned loading from an
adaptation with twelve presence differences. Of 45 paired timing trials, 40
complete and five VL-MOF processes time out on the deepest containment chain.
The result connects explicit structural meaning, verified checking and bounded
model reasoning. Parsing, native interchange, domain-query correctness, reflective
execution and general default/unset semantics remain outside the proved scope.
\keywords{EMOF \and Metamodeling \and Formal semantics \and Model conformance \and Mechanized verification}
\end{abstract}
