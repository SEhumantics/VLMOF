\begin{abstract}
Modeling languages, such as UML, commonly use metamodels to define their
abstract syntax. Essential Meta Object Facility (EMOF) provides a foundation
for metamodeling. Developing a formal conformance checker requires expressing
its semiformal structural requirements as mathematical predicates. We present
VL-MOF, a Lean formalization of a structural subset of EMOF with an
executable checker.
We prove that the checker accepts exactly the models satisfying our conformance
definitions. We also prove that translating named declarations preserves their
meaning under stated naming assumptions. We conduct experiments to compare validation results and loaded models with
those of Eclipse Modeling Framework validators and benchmark checking
performance across model sizes and containment structures.
\keywords{EMOF \and Metamodeling \and Formal semantics \and Model conformance \and Lean \and Mechanized verification}
\end{abstract}
