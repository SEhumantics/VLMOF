\section{Discussion}
\subsection{Related Work}
The exact comparison with prior formal metamodeling, conformance, and
textual-realization guarantees is pending. An unavailable artifact will
not be treated as evidence that a published guarantee is absent.
\subsection{Threats to Validity}
The principal open risks are source interpretation, interactions with
omitted features, the parser and adapter trust boundaries, and case
selection. The bounded metadata pilot must not be presented as full EMOF
self-description. These risks require evidence from the delivered artifact.
