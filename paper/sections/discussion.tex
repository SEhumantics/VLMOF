\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Related Work}
\paragraph{Formal semantics of modeling languages.}
Boronat and Meseguer give MOF an executable algebraic semantics
\cite{boronat_meseguer_2010}, but allow only the multiplicities $0..1$, $1..1$,
$0..\ast$ and $1..\ast$ and require opposites for composition
\cite{boronat_meseguer_report}; VL-MOF permits any finite bounds and
unpaired composition. Paige et al. separate formal conformance, specified in BON,
from fidelity to the intended metamodel \cite{paige_brooke_ostroff_2007}, and
MetaMorph formalizes the four-layer metamodeling stack \cite{doller_2022}.
Featherweight OCL gives OCL a machine-checked semantics in Isabelle/HOL
\cite{brucker_wolff_2012}; OCL constrains models,
whereas we formalize their structural conformance.

\paragraph{Mechanized model-driven engineering.}
Coq4MDE proves preservation for ISC model composition
\cite{kezadri_etal_2014} but compares opposites by membership,
where we count occurrences. K\"ohler et al. derive conditions under which graph
transformations preserve containment constraints
\cite{koehler_lewin_taentzer_2007}; we also separate repeated link occurrences
from distinct parents. CoqTL proof tactics \cite{cohen_tisi_douence_2024} and
Isabelle/HOL DSL construction \cite{brucker_tuong_wolff_2020} target model
transformation rather than conformance checking.

\paragraph{Textual metamodeling.}
NEREUS \cite{favre_duarte_2016} and KM3 \cite{jouault_bezivin_2006} specify
metamodels formally or textually, and Emfatic \cite{emfatic_docs} and Xcore
\cite{xcore_docs} give Ecore a textual syntax; our source documents also come
with a proved connection from their independent meaning to Core
(Theorem~\ref{thm:adequacy}).

\subsection{Threats to Validity}
\paragraph{Construct validity.}
The theorems concern our definitions, whose correspondence to EMOF rests on the
argument of Section~\ref{sec:conformance}. The subset assumes complete finite
snapshots and an implicit outer scope and restricts mixed-uniqueness opposites;
EMF agreement does not establish standards fidelity.

\paragraph{Internal validity.}
Proofs start at parsed source documents or Core; parsing, JSON, bridge and
measurement code is tested on selected cases, not proved. Ten warmups do not
establish JIT stabilization, and incomplete provenance (unhashed generation
sources, a dirty tree, partial hardware data) permits recomputing summaries, not
reproducing the build.

\paragraph{External and conclusion validity.}
Coverage excludes Real, structured datatypes, operations, reflection,
default/unset histories, comments and tags, richer derived or read-only
behavior, static features and derived unions, so results concern this structural
subset, not full EMOF. Train is the only public positive family, and unchanged
EMF Compare is outside the bridge subset. Synthetic workloads show structural
sensitivity, not industrial scalability, for the specified validators.
