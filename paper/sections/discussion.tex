\section{Discussion}
\subsection{What the connection adds}
The finite conformance semantics is useful beyond the current parser and
checker because it gives both implementations a common target. A new surface
language can be related to $\mathcal S$ and $E$; a new decision procedure can
be related to $C$. The catalog example shows why the split matters. Its
meaning is stated once in terms of inherited declarations and occurrence lists.
The source proof explains why alias resolution preserves that meaning, while
the checker proof explains why the final Boolean decides it.

The native study has a different role. It demonstrates how textual presence,
object identity, opposite maintenance, and ordering must be accounted for when
the formal observations meet a modeling framework. Its explicit sidecar makes
identity reallocation observable, and its rejection policy keeps unsupported
constructs visible. This is evidence about one bridge design, not a serializer
theorem.

\subsection{Related work}
Table~\ref{tab:prior} compares semantic domains and guarantees rather than
surface feature counts. Algebraic MOF supplies executable membership-equational
theories and reflective execution \cite{boronat_meseguer_2010}. Coq4MDE proves
that typed finite-multigraph composition preserves selected conformance
properties \cite{kezadri_etal_2014}. Isabelle UML/OCL work distinguishes
conservative theory generation from semantic preservation between source and
target interpretations \cite{brucker_tuong_wolff_2020}. Our operation is actual
alias-to-identity elaboration, and our target is a finite static EMOF profile.

In Coq4MDE, the composite predicate counts distinct source vertices in an

occurrences. Featherweight OCL formalizes an
OCL core with null and invalid \cite{brucker_wolff_2012}; our resolved finite

works establish that executable semantics, textual notation, and mechanized
preservation are established concerns; the contribution here is the particular
source-to-decision connection in Figure~\ref{fig:semantics}.

\subsection{Limits and next steps}
The standards connection rests on the documented source audit. Mechanization
establishes consequences of the selected predicates, while alternate readings
remain possible for static repeated references, nonnavigable incidence, and the
stronger occurrence-based containment choice. Extending the profile with
defaults, derived features, or richer datatypes will require revisiting absence,
observability, and value equality.

Equation~\ref{eq:adequacy} starts at a source AST. The parser, JSON decoder, EMF
runtime, datatype adaptation, and URI map production are executable trust
boundaries. The six public files all use one metamodel and adapted inputs, so
they show feasibility for that route rather than broad EMF compatibility.

A useful next step is to formalize one native decoding relation and prove it
preserves the same Core observations used here. A broader metadata account would
also need packages, associations, inheritance, and reflection before it could
support an EMOF self-description claim.
