\section{Discussion}
\subsection{Related Work}
Executable MOF semantics is an established foundation. Boronat and Meseguer
interpret a metamodel as a membership-equational theory, with model conformance,
reflective execution and EMF integration \cite{boronat_meseguer_2010}. Their
collection types include bags and sequences, so duplicate-sensitive collection
semantics is not itself new. Our contribution concerns the particular EMOF
source interpretation, its finite identity/occurrence predicates, and the checked
relationships among those predicates and their realization. The static domain
here is narrower than a full reflective account.

Coq4MDE establishes conformance preservation for model composition under
operation-specific hypotheses \cite{kezadri_etal_2014}. Its set-based opposite
relation compares edge membership, and its composite-ownership predicate counts
distinct source vertices. We likewise distinguish parent identity from occurrence
multiplicity, while explicitly retaining repeated values and separately bounding
active container-role identities. This is a comparison of semantic domains, not
a claim that the earlier framework cannot be extended. Its composition theorems
and our elaboration/checker theorems address different operations; neither implies
the other's full guarantee.

Mechanized UML/OCL provides further precedent for rigorous source interpretation.
Featherweight OCL treats null, invalid and collection operations
\cite{brucker_wolff_2012}; our resolved structural predicates do not implement
that expression language. Conservative theory generation in Isabelle UML/OCL
is also distinct from translation-semantic preservation
\cite{brucker_tuong_wolff_2020}. Equation~\ref{eq:adequacy} is a conditional
preservation/reflection statement between two specified meanings, not merely
logical consistency of generated declarations. Its standards-fidelity boundary
nevertheless remains the source audit.

NEREUS, KM3, Emfatic and Xcore show that formal or convenient textual
metamodeling is already well served
\cite{favre_duarte_2016,jouault_bezivin_2006,emfatic_docs,xcore_docs}.
CoqTL supplies reusable model-transformation proof infrastructure
\cite{cohen_tisi_douence_2024}. Our notation is an access route to the structural
foundation; a new syntax or proof assistant is not the novelty argument.
The comparison is targeted to these close precedents and does not establish a
literature-wide absence of equivalent results.

\subsection{Threats to Validity}
\paragraph{Construct validity.}
The principal risk is formalizing a defensible but unintended reading of EMOF.
Clause-level locators, explicit alternative readings and separating models expose
that risk. In particular, complete structural snapshots differ from states
reachable through reflective insertion; unpaired composites differ from explicit
opposite container roles. The implicit root-package convention is an abstraction,
not a stored reflective ownership graph. Source audits support these boundaries;
Lean cannot prove fidelity to English prose merely by accepting definitions.

\paragraph{Internal validity.}
The parser, JSON codec, Java bridge and measurement harness lie outside the
correspondence proofs. Native defaults, inverse maintenance and containment
reparenting can change the state before validation. A comparison therefore needs
retained inputs, loaded observations, declared normalization and diagnostics;
agreement of two booleans alone is insufficient. Separate schema and instance
phases prevent load success or schema rejection from being mistaken for instance
conformance. The runner records execution failure and not-run outcomes explicitly.

\paragraph{External validity.}
The selected Train case is one deliberately small projection, and the public
Train models are generated artifacts of the same metamodel. These do not establish
coverage of independent languages, industrial inputs or the full native ecosystem.
Domain queries and generated-code behavior are outside the structural claim.
The bridge additionally restricts datatypes, defaults and association-end forms.

\paragraph{Conclusion validity.}
Repeated positive validation measurements characterize the declared preloaded
workload and environment. They do not establish a general performance ranking,
and warmed repetitions in one process are not independent machine-level trials.
Startup, parsing, conversion and serialization are separate costs. Native validators
also enforce Ecore-specific rules beyond the common structural obligations;
semantic differences must be resolved or reported before interpreting timings.
Tests, rendering and kernel checking supply evidence for different properties;
none establishes standards fidelity on its own.
