\section{Discussion}
\subsection{Related Work}
\paragraph{Formal conformance and MOF semantics.}
Paige et al. specify the BON metamodel in PVS and Eiffel for conformance and
multiview consistency checking \cite{paige_brooke_ostroff_2007}. They distinguish
formal analysis from confidence that a specification captures the informal
metamodel. Boronat and Meseguer give executable MOF semantics through
membership-equational theories, including reflection and EMF integration
\cite{boronat_meseguer_2010}. Their bags and sequences already represent
duplicates. VL-MOF studies an EMOF~2.5.1 structural interpretation and proves
checker equivalence and symbolic elaboration for its identity/occurrence representation.

\paragraph{Containment and model changes.}
K\"ohler et al. constrain incoming containment edges and cycles in instance
graphs, and establish preservation conditions for graph transformations
\cite{koehler_lewin_taentzer_2007}. Our lists may retain repeated composite
occurrences from one parent; parent identity and active reverse properties are
checked separately. This is a representation difference, not a proved translation.
Coq4MDE proves composition preservation under operation-specific hypotheses
\cite{kezadri_etal_2014}; its opposites compare membership rather than occurrence
counts. Graph repair has separate correctness results for specified constraint
classes \cite{sandmann_2020}. Our checker reports violations but neither repairs
models nor proves preservation for composition or mutation.

\paragraph{Self-description.}
Thirioux et al. define an EMOF subset and present a promotion-based
metacircularity construction \cite{thirioux_etal_2007}. Their presentation is set-theoretic, with
Coq implementation reported as work in progress. Ordering, default initialization
and operational semantics are deferred. MetaMorph defines modeling languages in
typed first-order logic and establishes self-description for M2FOL
\cite{doller_2022}. These results exceed the self-description scope of our small
metadata interpreter, which checks a fixed vocabulary of class and scalar-attribute
objects.

\paragraph{Mechanized languages and textual notations.}
Featherweight OCL includes null, invalid and collection operations
\cite{brucker_wolff_2012}, which our resolved structural predicates omit.
Isabelle UML/OCL generates conservative theory extensions
\cite{brucker_tuong_wolff_2020}; VL-MOF's source adequacy theorem
(Equation~\ref{eq:adequacy}) instead relates symbolic satisfaction to actual Core
elaboration. CoqTL supplies transformation-proof infrastructure
\cite{cohen_tisi_douence_2024}. NEREUS, KM3, Emfatic and Xcore establish precedents
for textual metamodeling
\cite{favre_duarte_2016,jouault_bezivin_2006,emfatic_docs,xcore_docs}.
The DSL is an interface to our formalization, not a claim of novelty in notation.

\subsection{Threats to Validity}
\paragraph{Construct validity.}
The principal risk is an unintended reading of EMOF. Clause references,
alternative readings and distinguishing examples expose this risk, but kernel
checking cannot prove fidelity to normative prose. The model describes complete
static snapshots, not reflective reachability, and uses an implicit outer scope.
The published Train diagram and pinned executable artifact also differ in
multiplicity; their profiles must not be conflated.

\paragraph{Internal validity.}
Parsing, JSON conversion, the Java bridge and the measurement harness are outside
the proofs. Defaults, inverse maintenance and reparenting may change loaded
state. We therefore compare retained observations as well as validation results.
Exact historical orchestration/generation sources were not hashed; retained
inputs, commands and raw samples support result recomputation, not complete
historical build reproduction.

\paragraph{External and conclusion validity.}
Train is the only public positive family; EMF Compare supplies an unsupported
case. Controlled sizes and shapes establish neither industrial breadth,
authoring productivity nor domain-query correctness. Repeated measurements
describe the declared environment, not asymptotic complexity or a general speed
ranking. Ecore-specific restrictions and the selected generic validator limit
transfer to other tools and generated validators.
