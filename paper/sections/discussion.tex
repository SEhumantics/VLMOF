\section{Discussion}
\subsection{Related work and the scope of the guarantee}
Boronat and Meseguer give an algebraic MOF semantics using membership-equational
theories and an executable Maude/EMF realization, including reflective
bootstrapping \cite{boronat_meseguer_2010}. Their model-conformance account relates
models to terms of the generated theory. This establishes that a connected,
executable MOF foundation and metamodel-as-data are not novel by themselves.
Our result instead concerns a particular finite EMOF 2.5.1 interpretation and
actual symbolic elaboration into an independently defined checked Core. We have
not established a featurewise equivalence between the two semantic domains.

Coq4MDE formalizes typed finite multigraphs and proves preservation properties
of model composition \cite{kezadri_etal_2014}. Its published Theorems 4--6 concern
multiplicity, opposites and composite references under hypotheses on the
\emph{bind} operation. The lower-bound and composite-reference results use
compatibility, freshness and injectivity; the opposite theorem reports no such
residual premises. Its \emph{areComposite} predicate counts distinct typed source
vertices in an edge-set comprehension.
Our elaboration theorem quantifies over a source document and its alias-to-ID
elaboration. Our incoming-composite measure counts raw reference occurrences,
so a repeated occurrence contributes repeatedly. These are
different operations and abstractions. This distinction does not establish
subsumption, nor a claim of the first mechanized conformance preservation.

The Isabelle UML/OCL textual realization is the closest comparison at the
language-tool boundary \cite{brucker_tuong_wolff_2020}. Its central certification
is conservativity of generated theory extensions. That article explicitly
distinguishes logical safety from semantic preservation, which requires
interpretations of source and target in a common domain. Equation~\ref{eq:adequacy}
connects separately stated source satisfaction to a concrete target conformance
predicate and composes with Equation~\ref{eq:checker}. The new assertion is this
particular connection under explicit lexical admissibility, not the
safety of executing generated prover commands. Featherweight OCL addresses
another boundary: a mechanized OCL core with null and invalid
\cite{brucker_wolff_2012}. Our Boolean static predicates over resolved finite
records neither implement OCL's exceptional-value semantics nor replace that work.

NEREUS offers textual formal metamodeling with a constructive translation to
CASL \cite{favre_duarte_2016}; KM3 provides a compact metamodel specification
language \cite{jouault_bezivin_2006}. Emfatic and Xcore provide practical Ecore
syntax and tool integration \cite{emfatic_docs,xcore_docs}. These works already
address authoring and translation. We do not infer the absence of additional
formal guarantees from a documentation page or the sections inspected here.
CoqTL proof tactics likewise demonstrate model-transformation proof automation
\cite{cohen_tisi_douence_2024}; their transformation properties should not be
confused with our source-binding and checker contract.

\subsection{Threats to validity and remaining obligations}
The strongest internal evidence is kernel checking of the Lean proofs. A proof
of our predicate does not prove that it faithfully captures every normative
clause. The source audit therefore remains a separate argument, especially for
creation-valid bounds, repeated references, nonnavigable incidence and the
relationship between static snapshots and reflective operations. Deferred
features can interact with retained ones: defaults affect absence, derived
features affect observability, and richer datatypes affect value equality.
Extending the domain requires revisiting those interactions, not merely adding
record fields.

The source theorem begins at an AST. Parsing, lexical conventions, JSON decoding,
EMF loading, datatype adaptation and identity-map production remain trusted
code. Native URI mappings are location dependent and producer supplied. A
successful round trip establishes agreement under that recorded mapping for
that run; it is not a universal serializer theorem or evidence of unchanged
original-file meaning after adaptation. The public-case modifications are
therefore listed explicitly and their originals retained.

Case selection is narrow. The six Train files share one metamodel and are not
six independent language designs. Their names \emph{inject} and \emph{repair}
do not determine validity under our chosen structural fragment. EMF Compare
exercises rejection of a richer metamodel; it is not a successful compatibility
case. Authored tests target particular distinctions and do not estimate the
frequency of bridge errors in unknown models. Runtime observations on one host
cannot support cross-tool superiority claims.

The metadata interpreter remains a fixed pilot rather than a reflective tower.
General self-description, complete EMOF behavior and richer native compatibility
remain future obligations. Completing these features would require new source
interpretations, representations and preservation arguments; the current
results should be used only within the declared fragment.
