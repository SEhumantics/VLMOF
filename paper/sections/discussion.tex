\section{Discussion}
\subsection{Related Work}
Executable MOF semantics is established. Boronat and Meseguer interpret
metamodels as membership-equational theories with conformance, reflective
execution and EMF integration \cite{boronat_meseguer_2010}. Their bags and
sequences already support duplicate-sensitive collections. Our narrower result is
a clause-audited EMOF 2.5.1 structural profile with identity- and
occurrence-sensitive predicates, a checked finite decision procedure, and
conditional source-to-Core correspondence.

Thirioux et al. define a typed-graph EMOF subset and validate its
metacircularity by promoting an EMOF model to its reference model
\cite{thirioux_etal_2007}. Their development is set-theoretic; Coq implementation
is reported as work in progress. Promotion supplies structural predicates,
while ordering, default initialization and operational semantics remain deferred.
Our checker equivalence and conditional symbolic correspondence concern different
contracts; the bounded metadata pilot establishes no EMOF self-description.

Later Coq4MDE work proves conformance preservation for model composition under
operation-specific hypotheses \cite{kezadri_etal_2014}. Its set-based opposite
relation compares membership and its composite predicate counts distinct source
vertices. We instead retain repeated occurrences, distinguish parent identity
from occurrence multiplicity, and separately bound active container roles. These
are different semantic domains and operations: our checker/elaboration results
do not imply composition preservation, and Coq4MDE's theorems do not entail this
particular EMOF interpretation.

Mechanized UML/OCL is further precedent. Featherweight OCL includes null,
invalid and collection operations \cite{brucker_wolff_2012}, which our resolved
structural predicates omit. Isabelle UML/OCL's conservative theory generation
adds no new theorems in the original theory's signature and preserves consistency
when that original theory is consistent \cite{brucker_tuong_wolff_2020}.
Equation~\ref{eq:adequacy}, by contrast, preserves and reflects satisfaction
between a symbolic instance and its actual Core elaboration. Neither result is a
substitute for the other.

NEREUS, KM3, Emfatic and Xcore also show that textual metamodeling is not new
\cite{favre_duarte_2016,jouault_bezivin_2006,emfatic_docs,xcore_docs}; CoqTL
supplies reusable transformation-proof infrastructure
\cite{cohen_tisi_douence_2024}. The contribution is neither a new syntax nor the
choice of Lean. These targeted comparisons do not support a claim that equivalent
formalization is absent from the literature.

\subsection{Threats to Validity}
\paragraph{Construct validity.}
The central risk is a defensible but unintended EMOF reading. Clause locators,
alternative readings and separating witnesses expose that risk, but kernel
checking cannot prove fidelity to normative prose. The model covers complete
static snapshots, not reflective reachability; it distinguishes unpaired
composites from explicit opposite container roles and uses an implicit root scope
rather than a stored reflective ownership graph.

\paragraph{Internal validity.}
The parser, JSON codec, Java bridge and measurement harness are outside the Lean
correspondence proofs. Native default handling, inverse maintenance and
containment reparenting may alter a loaded state before validation. Accordingly,
the evaluation retains inputs, loaded observations, normalization records and
diagnostics; matching acceptance booleans is insufficient. Schema and instance
validation, loading and normalization are reported as separate phases.

\paragraph{External and conclusion validity.}
Train is the only public positive family; the unchanged EMF Compare case is a
public unsupported boundary, not positive transfer evidence. Controlled stars,
chains and repeated railway groups explore selected shapes and sizes, not
industrial representativeness, author productivity, domain queries or generated
code behavior. Independent processes and counterbalanced order characterize the
declared environment and workload only. Repetitions are not additional models,
and separate startup, parsing, conversion and serialization costs support neither
a general performance ranking nor an asymptotic claim. Native validators impose
Ecore-specific rules, so disagreement and state-alignment failures remain results
to explain rather than noise to remove.
