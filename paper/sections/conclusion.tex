\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
VL-MOF expresses a structural subset of EMOF as conformance predicates with
proved connections to checking and named declarations. The guarantees apply
to this subset, including its opposite-count restriction and naming assumptions.
EMF comparisons distinguish validation agreement from loaded-state agreement;
synthetic workloads identify deep containment as a practical limit.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Future extensions include mixed-uniqueness projection and reflective operations
with conformance-preservation guarantees. Supporting metamodels as models and
EMOF self-description would connect metadata interpretation to these semantics.
