\section{Conclusion}
VL-MOF connects a source-audited structural EMOF interpretation to a proved
finite checker and conditional symbolic correspondence. Its opposite-count
consequence excludes repeated-link edits without assuming forward uniqueness.
Native comparisons separate acceptance from loaded-state alignment; controlled
workloads expose size and shape limits of this implementation.

Further work requires additional contracts: transition and preservation results
for reflection, richer observation states for defaults/unset history, and
packages, inheritance, references and materialized ownership for full metadata
interpretation. The static results provide a basis for these extensions without
establishing them.
