\section{Conclusion}
VL-MOF gives structural EMOF conformance a precise meaning, a verified checker
and a proved connection to symbolic declarations. Its treatment of identities,
repeated values and containment supports validation, an opposite-count constraint
and a conformance-preserving Boolean edit.
Native comparisons reveal loading differences; measurements show sensitivity to
model size and containment depth.

Further work will address graph-changing operations, richer
observations for defaults and unset state, and metadata interpretation with
packages, inheritance, references and explicit ownership.
