\section{Conclusion}
VL-MOF gives a selected structural EMOF fragment precise semantics, a verified
checker and proved symbolic elaboration. Identity, occurrence and containment
reasoning supports an opposite-count constraint and a Boolean edit. The selected
EMF comparison exposes loading differences; costs depend on size and depth.
Further work concerns graph-changing edits, defaults and unset state, and
metadata with packages, inheritance, references and explicit ownership.
