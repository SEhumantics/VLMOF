\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
VL-MOF expresses a structural subset of EMOF as conformance predicates with
proved connections to checking and to source documents.
EMF comparisons separate validation from loaded-state agreement;
synthetic workloads identify deep containment as a practical limit. For future
work, we plan to expand the work to cover the full EMOF specification, with
features like mixed-uniqueness opposites, reflective operations, and EMOF
self-description.
