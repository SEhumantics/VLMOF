\section{Conclusion}
VL-MOF gives a selected structural part of EMOF~2.5.1 explicit conformance
conditions and an executable checker in Lean. Its standards analysis explains
choices that change the admitted models, while its proofs connect checker
acceptance to those conditions and symbolic declarations to the Core
representation under explicit naming premises. The full structural Train case
exercises that connection through the implementation. EMF comparisons show that
agreement must be assessed on both validation decisions and loaded state;
controlled workloads also reveal limits at greater containment depth. Together,
these results support checking and reasoning from a common structural semantics.
Establishing formal correspondence for file interchange would extend the current
guarantees beyond the AST and Core boundary.
