\section{Conclusion}
VL-MOF gives one answer to the question posed at the start: for a lexically
admissible source AST in the selected structural profile, acceptance after
actual elaboration is equivalent to its independently defined symbolic meaning.
The result is supported by a reusable finite conformance semantics and a
complete executable decision. The catalog example shows the connection across
inherited identity, opposite occurrences, containment, and absence. A restricted
EMF route provides evidence that these observations can be retained through
native resources when identity and adaptation are explicit. The remaining work
is to extend the semantic domain and prove more of that realization boundary.
