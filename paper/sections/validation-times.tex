Each completed cell summarizes 150 measured samples across five processes.
For the 501-object chain, all EMF processes completed; every VL-MOF process
exceeded 120 seconds before emitting its buffered samples. No VL-MOF samples
are retained for that cell. The limit covers 40 schema-only and 40 combined
iterations plus process overhead, rather than one validation. The table treats
processes as independent units; pooled summaries remain in the artifact.

Graph shape matters as well as size: at 101 objects, VL-MOF's chain median is
about eleven times its star median despite fewer observation rows. EMF medians
remain below 3 ms for these inputs. VL-MOF scans raw lists during graph expansion
and checks containment reachability per object, which may explain depth
sensitivity. These measurements neither isolate a bottleneck nor establish a
complexity bound or a general speed ranking between the implementations.
