Each process median uses 30 measured samples after ten warmups; each completed
cell therefore contains 150 samples per tool. For the 501-object chain,
all five EMF processes completed and supply the reported 150 samples. Each
VL-MOF benchmark process exceeded its 120-second process limit before emitting
its buffered sample array, so there are no retained VL-MOF samples for that
cell. The limit covers 40 schema-only and 40 combined iterations plus
process overhead, not a single validation. Pooled sample summaries remain in
the archive; Table~\ref{tab:validation-times} treats the process as the independent unit.

The results show sensitivity to graph shape as well as size. At 101 objects,
VL-MOF's chain median is about eleven times its star median, although the chain
has fewer observation rows. EMF remains below 3 ms at the reported median for
these inputs. These descriptive measurements compare the current implementations,
whose data structures and runtimes differ. The checker repeatedly scans raw
lists for graph expansion and tests containment reachability per object; depth
can increase that work. We have not isolated a measured bottleneck or proved
a complexity bound. These are not general speed rankings; loading, parsing,
normalization, diagnostics and startup lie outside the measured window.
