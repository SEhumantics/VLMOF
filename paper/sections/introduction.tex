\section{Introduction}
Model-driven software engineering uses metamodels to define the structures of
modeling languages. Essential MOF (EMOF) supplies a shared vocabulary of classes,
properties, inheritance and associations \cite{omg_mof_251,omg_uml_25}.
A reusable structural semantics should explain which declarations are legal,
which finite object structures they admit, and which consequences follow across
supported metamodels.

The difficulty is partly interpretive. EMOF combines restrictions, adopted UML
definitions and reflective-service clauses. A class-creation prerequisite need
not be a structural restriction; repeated references need not denote distinct
containers. A checker can correctly decide a predicate that resolves these
questions incorrectly. Source justification and implementation correctness need
separate evidence. Native comparison adds another boundary: tools may accept
states whose values changed during loading.

Substantial precedents include executable algebraic MOF conformance
\cite{boronat_meseguer_2010}, promotion-based EMOF self-description
\cite{thirioux_etal_2007}, conditional composition preservation in Coq4MDE
\cite{kezadri_etal_2014}, and conservative theory generation in Isabelle UML/OCL
\cite{brucker_tuong_wolff_2020}. Our contribution concerns the following
combination of source-audited identity/occurrence semantics, decisions over
malformed raw inputs, and symbolic-to-Core correspondence.

We investigate three research questions:
\begin{description}
\item[RQ1.] Which source interpretations change admitted schemas or snapshots,
and what justifies the selected readings?
\item[RQ2.] What reusable consequences and executable/authoring correspondence
follow from this interpretation?
\item[RQ3.] What do the Train projection, native comparisons and controlled
workloads establish about practical scope and cost?
\end{description}

VL-MOF contributes (i) an EMOF 2.5.1 structural interpretation with separating
examples; (ii) checker equivalence, conditional symbolic correspondence and a
reusable opposite-count consequence in Lean; and (iii) an executable realization
with a declared EMF evaluation. We study finite structural snapshots;
reflective execution, general default/unset
state and full self-description remain deferred. Generality over represented
schemas establishes neither empirical breadth nor domain safety.

Section~2 motivates the account; Section~3 develops definitions and proofs.
Sections~4--5 present the realization and evaluation, followed by related work,
threats and conclusions.
