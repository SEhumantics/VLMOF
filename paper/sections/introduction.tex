\section{Introduction}
Metamodel authors need to know which object structures their declarations admit.
A model may load successfully in an editor yet fail a multiplicity constraint;
two validators may accept inputs whose values changed during loading. A reusable
structural semantics gives validation, model reasoning and interchange analysis
a common object of study. Essential MOF (EMOF) provides the metamodeling vocabulary,
but deriving that semantics requires relating its restrictions, adopted UML
definitions and reflective-service requirements \cite{omg_mof_251,omg_uml_25}.

Containment illustrates the problem: repeated references can name one parent,
while two container properties can both name that parent. MOF distinguishes
these obligations. A checker proof alone cannot establish that its predicate
selects the right specification context.

Formal MOF semantics already has substantial precedents. Boronat and Meseguer
provide executable algebraic conformance and reflective machinery
\cite{boronat_meseguer_2010}; Coq4MDE proves composition preservation under explicit
conditions \cite{kezadri_etal_2014}; Isabelle UML/OCL supplies conservative theory
generation \cite{brucker_tuong_wolff_2020}. We contribute an inspectable structural
interpretation of EMOF 2.5.1 that connects source decisions, identity- and
occurrence-sensitive constraints, a proved finite checker, and conditional
correspondence with independently defined symbolic metamodels and instances.
The incremental result is this checked semantic contract and its demonstrated
consequences, rather than Lean, syntax or collection support alone.

We ask: \textbf{RQ1}, which source interpretations change admitted declarations
or snapshots; \textbf{RQ2}, what reasoning and authoring/checking guarantees follow;
and \textbf{RQ3}, what the executable examples and native-state comparisons
establish about practical scope and cost. We answer through selected definitions
and a proof, source-derived separating cases, a railway edit scenario, and a
corpus that retains unsupported and misaligned inputs. Mathematical generality
over represented schemas is kept separate from empirical breadth.
