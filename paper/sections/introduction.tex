\section{Introduction}
Metamodels define modeling languages' concepts and structural constraints.
MOF provides a common basis, including for UML's metamodel; EMOF supplies its
essential class-modeling constructs \cite{omg_mof_251,omg_uml_25}.
Formalizing them supports conformance checking and reasoning about admitted models.

EMOF combines restrictions, adopted UML definitions and reflective-service
clauses. A creation prerequisite need not restrict every static snapshot, and
repeated references need not identify different containers. Proving a checker
correct therefore leaves a separate obligation: justifying its interpretation
of the standard.

Previous work provides executable MOF semantics, formal model composition and
mechanized UML/OCL \cite{boronat_meseguer_2010,kezadri_etal_2014,brucker_tuong_wolff_2020}.
These developments address different representations and operations. We examine
structural conformance with explicit declaration identities and repeated values,
and connect symbolic declarations to that representation through verified
elaboration. The questions are:
\begin{description}
\item[RQ1.] Which EMOF interpretations affect conformance, and how are they justified?
\item[RQ2.] Which checking, elaboration and model properties can be proved?
\item[RQ3.] What do native comparisons and controlled workloads establish about
applicability and cost?
\end{description}

We present \textbf{VL-MOF}, a Lean formalization of structural EMOF with three
contributions: (1) definitions of schema well-formedness and snapshot conformance,
with source-based explanations of consequential choices; (2) proofs of checker
correctness and symbolic elaboration, together with an opposite-link constraint and
a conformance-preserving Boolean edit; and (3) an implementation evaluated on Train models and controlled
workloads against a declared EMF profile. The formalization concerns finite
snapshots; reflective execution, general default/unset state and full
self-description are outside its scope.

The remainder of the paper is organized as follows. Section~2 motivates the
work. Section~3 presents the formalization, Section~4 its implementation, and
Section~5 the evaluation. Sections~6 and~7 discuss related work, limitations
and conclusions.
