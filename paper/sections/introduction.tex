\section{Introduction}
Metamodels define a modeling language's elements, properties and relationships.
The Meta Object Facility (MOF) provides a common foundation for these definitions,
including UML's metamodel. Essential MOF (EMOF) is its essential compliance level
\cite{omg_mof_251,omg_uml_25}. Interpreting these shared constructs consistently
matters: tools may otherwise disagree on whether the same model conforms.

EMOF distributes requirements across structural restrictions, adopted UML
definitions and reflective services. Turning them into conformance conditions
requires distinguishing constraints on model states from prerequisites of
operations. Proving a checker correct therefore leaves a separate obligation:
justifying the definitions against the standard. We ask:
\begin{description}
\item[RQ1.] Which structural EMOF interpretations affect conformance, and how
are they justified from the specification?
\item[RQ2.] What guarantees connect symbolic declarations, formal conformance
and executable checking?
\item[RQ3.] Where does the implementation agree with a specified EMF validation
setup, and how does validation time vary across controlled workloads?
\end{description}

We present \textbf{VL-MOF}, a Lean formalization with three contributions:
(1) schema and snapshot conformance definitions with source-based explanations
of consequential choices; (2) checker soundness and completeness, and a proved
correspondence between independent symbolic semantics and elaboration;
(3) a tool evaluated through the full structural Train case, EMF comparisons
and controlled workloads. A derived opposite-link constraint illustrates reasoning
from the definitions. The scope is finite structural snapshots; reflective
execution and general default/unset histories require further semantics.
