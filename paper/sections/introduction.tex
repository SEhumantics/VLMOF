\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}
Metamodels define a modeling language's abstract syntax: its kinds of elements,
properties and relationships. The Meta Object Facility (MOF), including its
Essential MOF (EMOF) compliance level, provides a common foundation
\cite{omg_mof_251}. To obtain a verified conformance checker, these requirements
must be expressed over a precise model representation and connected to an
executable decision procedure.

Formal conformance and executable MOF semantics have established precedents
\cite{boronat_meseguer_2010,paige_brooke_ostroff_2007}. Building on them, we focus
on connecting a structural subset of EMOF to source documents with an
independent meaning, verified translation and executable checking. A
\emph{source document} is the parsed form of what a user writes: declarations
and objects that refer to each other by name. This raises three questions:
\begin{description}
\item[RQ1.] How are the selected structural EMOF requirements represented as
conformance predicates, and what restrictions does this formalization impose?
\item[RQ2.] What guarantees connect source documents, formal conformance
and executable checking?
\item[RQ3.] Where does the implementation agree with a specified EMF validation
setup, and how does validation time vary across controlled workloads?
\end{description}

We present \textbf{VL-MOF}, a Lean \cite{demoura_ullrich_2021} formalization of
a structural subset of EMOF with executable conformance checking. First, we
define metamodel well-formedness and model conformance, documenting the
requirements they adapt and our additional restrictions. Second, we prove the
checker equivalent to conformance and elaboration faithful to the independent
meaning of source documents under explicit naming conditions. Third, an adapted
railway case study and controlled workloads evaluate validation decisions,
loaded values and checking cost.

motivates the work; Section~\ref{sec:formalization} presents the formalized

Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} performs evaluation; Section~\ref{sec:discussion}

concludes.
